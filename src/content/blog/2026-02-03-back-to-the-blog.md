Well, it's been a while! After some gentle ribbing from a friend who will remain nameless, I've decided it's time to get back to this site and start blogging again.

Since my last post in 2017, a lot has changed both in my personal life and in the tech world. I've moved on to working at CLHbid.com, have two wonderful kids, and have taken time to work on myself both personally and professionally. What I haven't done is much blogging or side projects. Who knew family would take up so much time?!

Over the next few months I'll be using this blog as a space to try out some fresh ideas and technologies such as Astro and BlueSky/ATProto. I've also been using Claude Code to do a lot more "vibe coding" and I'm looking forward to sharing some insights and experiences around that. I'll also share code snippets and knowledge of use to me, for my reference and with the hope it will be useful to others.

Stay tuned!
